Toronto customs — CARM era

CBSA CARM went fully live in 2024 with RPP importer accounts. We work with our own Toronto customs broker who files with CBSA on clients' behalf, with HST 13% (5% GST + 8% Ontario PST) collected and prepaid — all included in the DDP quote.

GTA clearance throughput is high; general cargo is stable. Frozen / agricultural via CFIA, cosmetics via Health Canada — DDP means all fees are prepaid; Markham / Richmond Hill / Scarborough recipients sign and that's it.

Last-mile is on our own fleet: Markham / RH / Scarborough same-day or next-day; Mississauga / Brampton 1 day; GTA outskirts (Hamilton / Oakville) 1–2 days.

Shipping to Toronto · FAQ

01 How long does sea freight from China to Toronto take?
Sea is 28–32 days door-to-door via Halifax direct, or 30–35 days via Vancouver + rail. Air 7–10 days via Pearson Airport.
02 Can you deliver to Markham / Richmond Hill / Scarborough homes?
Yes — these are the three GTA Chinese communities and our main Toronto-lane delivery clusters. Own truck 1–2 days direct to residential.
03 Does the Toronto quote include HST?
Yes. Under DDP, GST + Ontario HST (13% combined) are all collected and prepaid by us. Markham / Toronto recipient signs and that's it.
04 How do UofT / York students ship arrival luggage?
Air+Truck 8–10 days to dorm door. UofT St. George / Scarborough / Mississauga campuses, York Keele campus — just give the dorm address.
05 Can you deliver to Toronto-area FBA Canada?
Yes. YYZ4 (Mississauga), YYZ9 (Bolton) and other GTA FBA Canada warehouses — DDP with HST, appointment intake handled.
06 Can you ship frozen food to Markham?
Yes — routine on our Toronto lane. -18°C cold chain + CFIA filing throughout, Markham / Richmond Hill / Scarborough Chinese-community direct.
