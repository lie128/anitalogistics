London customs — clearing post-Brexit

Since 2021 the UK has cleared independently of the EU. After Felixstowe / Southampton arrival, our UK customs broker files with HMRC: ≤£135 via IOSS (VAT prepaid by sender); >£135 standard import declaration with VAT prepaid.

Under DDP, VAT and duties are all covered by us. Recipients at a UCL dorm, Soho restaurant, or Colindale home sign the package and that's it — true door-to-door with no follow-up paperwork.

Cosmetics require UKCA registration; food items (incl. Chinese seasonings) via CHED filing; wine via excise channels. We have all of these mapped — clients don't deal with paperwork.

Shipping to London · FAQ

01 How long does sea freight from China to London take?
Sea is 30–35 days door-to-door (FCL direct to Felixstowe / SOU / LGW); LCL 32–38 days. Air 7–10 days via Heathrow direct.
02 Can you deliver to UCL / LSE / Imperial dorms in London?
Yes. Give us the dorm address + room number + recipient name — Air+Truck 8–11 days to door, signed by dorm reception. Books / bedding / rice cookers / seasonings all accepted.
03 Does the London quote include VAT?
Yes. Under DDP, VAT and duties are prepaid. ≤£135 via IOSS, >£135 via standard import declaration. Recipient signs in London — no follow-up charges.
04 Can you restock Soho Chinatown restaurants?
Yes. Seasonings, dried goods, hot-pot base, tea at ambient via DDP; frozen items (dumplings / seafood) on -18°C cold chain. Soho / Bayswater / Camden restaurants all delivered.
05 Can you deliver to London-area FBA UK warehouses?
Yes. LBA4 / MAN1 / EUK5 / BHX4 etc. — DDP intake with VAT, appointment + labeling + declaration all handled.
06 Can I ship wine / cosmetics to London?
Yes. Wine via excise channels (with duty); cosmetics need UKCA registration — major brands routine, DDP with VAT.
