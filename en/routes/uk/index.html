UK customs — how we clear post-Brexit

Since the UK left the EU customs union in 2021, UK clearance is independent. We work with our own UK customs broker and HMRC declaration channels: ≤£135 via IOSS (VAT prepaid by sender); >£135 via standard import declaration with VAT and duties prepaid.

Under DDP, VAT and duties are all paid by us — recipients in London / Manchester / Edinburgh sign the package, no follow-up paperwork or charges.

Food / cosmetics / pharmacy have dedicated compliance channels. Infant goods, formula, supplements — all routine; clients don't need to handle the paperwork.

Shipping to United Kingdom · FAQ

01 How long does sea freight from China to the UK take?
Shenzhen to UK by sea is roughly 30–35 days door-to-door (FCL direct to Felixstowe / Southampton); LCL is 32–38 days. Air is 7–12 days. Times include declaration + customs + last-mile; peak season adds 3–7 days.
02 Does the China-to-UK quote include VAT?
Yes. On DDP, both VAT and duties are prepaid by us. ≤£135 via IOSS, >£135 via standard import declaration. The UK recipient signs and that's it — no further charges.
03 How do students ship luggage to UK dorms?
Our Air+Truck line lands at the dorm in 8–12 days. Give us the dorm address + room number + recipient name and the truck pre-books delivery time. Books, bedding, rice cookers, seasonings, clothes all accepted.
04 Did Brexit change UK customs clearance?
Yes — UK customs separated from the EU in 2021 and requires independent declaration. We work with our own UK customs broker on HMRC channels (IOSS / OSS / standard import), and DDP duties + VAT prepaid is unchanged for clients.
05 Can you deliver to Amazon FBA UK warehouses?
Yes. LBA4 / MAN1 / EUK5 / BHX4 — all routine. DDP intake with VAT, appointment + labeling + declaration handled end-to-end.
06 Can I ship wine / cosmetics / pharmacy to the UK?
Yes. Wine via DDP with VAT; cosmetics require UKCA / cosmetic compliance — all major brands routine; OTC pharmacy / supplements via dedicated channels.
07 How do I get a China-to-UK quote?
Add WeChat (anitaleung8888) and send a photo + dimensions + destination city. Quote returned within 5 minutes — VAT and last-mile included, no follow-up charges at the door.
