LA customs — LA / LB port advantages

LA / Long Beach is the largest US container-port cluster, with high CBP throughput and abundant broker resources. We file via our own customs broker and West Coast ISF channels — clearance is stable, holds are rare.

California-specific compliance (Prop 65 labeling on certain goods, food / cosmetic / electronics rules) is handled by us. Frozen food via FDA channels, cosmetics via FDA registration, electronics via FCC — DDP means all duties / fees are prepaid.

Last-mile delivery is on our own trucks: SGV / Orange County same-day or next-day; Inland Empire 1–2 days; remote (Palm Springs / Big Bear) 3–4 days.

Yes. Arcadia / Monterey Park / Rowland Heights / Alhambra / Temple City — our own trucks deliver 1–2 days to residential addresses. Upstairs delivery negotiable.
