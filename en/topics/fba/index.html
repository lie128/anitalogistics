Short answer: ship FBA inbound on DDP, prep handled end-to-end

FBA inbound is the leg from China to Amazon's overseas warehouse. We ship DDP: export declaration, international transit, import customs, duties, and truck to the FBA warehouse — plus appointment booking, labeling, and declaration, all handled. Sellers do not need to coordinate trucking or customs; we work to your replenishment schedule.

Why ship DDP

FBA sellers dread surprise taxes mid-transit, or rejection over non-compliant declaration / labeling. Under DDP duties are prepaid by us, appointments and labels follow Amazon rules, and clearance is steady for general cargo with low hold rates — so goods reach the warehouse and go live smoothly.

How pricing works

FBA freight moves with mode, season, destination warehouse, and weight/volume, so a fixed list misleads. Quotes are given in real time on WeChat (within 5 minutes) after we see the goods and destination — transparent, no hidden fees. Book capacity early for Q4 peak.

FAQ

01 How do I ship Amazon FBA inbound?
From Shenzhen to Amazon warehouses in the US / UK / Germany via sea, Sea+Truck, air, or Air+Truck — DDP throughout, including appointment booking, labeling, declaration, and truck to the warehouse. WeChat me the destination warehouse and cargo details to arrange it.
02 Sea or air for FBA inbound?
Bulk, non-urgent stock is cheapest by sea or Sea+Truck (US 25–30 days / UK 30–35 days); urgent, mid-volume restock goes by air or Air+Truck (7–12 days). It depends on your replenishment timing and budget.
03 Does FBA prep include labeling and appointment booking?
Yes. FNSKU / carton marks / compliance labels are applied to Amazon spec, and delivery slots are booked per platform rules to avoid rejection.
04 What does FBA DDP (duties prepaid) mean?
DDP means export declaration, import clearance, duties (VAT included for the UK), and truck delivery are all prepaid by us; goods go straight into the FBA warehouse with no extra tax for the seller.
05 Can you ship to UK / Germany FBA warehouses?
Yes. UK FBA includes VAT prepaid; Germany / EU FBA are also routine — sea or air DDP intake, including declaration and appointment booking.
