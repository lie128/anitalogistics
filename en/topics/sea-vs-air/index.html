Remember this first

Choosing a mode is essentially a trade-off between speed and cost, matched to your cargo. Faster costs more (air); slower costs less (sea). Sea+Truck and Air+Truck are the middle options that bundle transit with door delivery by truck. Below is who each one suits.

Who each mode suits

Sea (FCL / LCL)
Cheapest. Best for furniture, appliances, bulky goods, stocking; even one or two furniture pieces can go LCL
Sea + Truck
Same transit as sea, but the truck delivers to your door after port arrival. Best for furniture and bulk e-commerce replenishment
Air (from 12 KG)
Fastest, 7–12 days. Best for cosmetics, electronics, urgent or valuable small batches; slots even in peak season
Air + Truck
Cheaper than couriers (DHL/UPS), faster than sea. Best for student luggage and mid-volume e-commerce

Choosing by scenario

Moving / stocking / furniture
Large volume, not urgent → Sea or Sea+Truck, cheapest
Urgent / valuable / small batch
Cosmetics, electronics, gifts, documents → Air
Student luggage
Mid volume, want it fast and duty-prepaid to the dorm → Air+Truck
Mid-volume e-commerce / FBA
Balance speed and cost → Sea+Truck or Air+Truck

How volumetric weight works (where people overpay)

Freight is charged on chargeable weight — the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight. Sea volumetric KG = L×W×H (cm) / 6000; air uses / 5000 or / 6000 depending on lane. So large-but-light cargo (bedding, foam-heavy packaging) is usually billed by volume — compressing the volume when packing saves money. A photo-based quote is most accurate.

All four ship DDP (duties prepaid)

Whichever you pick, it is DDP throughout: export clearance + import customs + duties + truck to the door, all included. The recipient just signs — no customs chasing extra tax on arrival.

FAQ

01 Sea or air — which is more economical for shipping abroad?
It depends on time and cargo. Large, non-urgent bulky stocking (furniture, appliances) is cheapest by sea, 25–45 days door-to-door; urgent, valuable, small batches (cosmetics, electronics) are fastest by air, 7–12 days. Both ship DDP door-to-door.
02 What do "Sea+Truck" and "Air+Truck" mean?
Sea+Truck = sea freight to port, then local truck to the recipient's door — good for furniture and bulk e-commerce. Air+Truck = air express plus destination trucking — cheaper than couriers, faster than sea — good for student luggage and mid-volume e-commerce.
03 Is there a minimum for sea freight?
No. Sea LCL accepts even one or two furniture pieces; air has a 12 KG minimum; Taobao consolidation has no minimum count.
04 Is freight charged by weight or volume?
By chargeable weight — the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight. Sea volumetric KG = L×W×H (cm) / 6000; air uses / 5000 or / 6000. Large-but-light cargo is usually billed by volume, so compressing the packing saves money.
05 Can all four modes be duty-prepaid to the door?
Yes. Sea, Sea+Truck, Air, and Air+Truck all ship DDP: export clearance, import customs, duties, and truck delivery included — the recipient just signs, no further charges.
