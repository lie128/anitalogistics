Why sensitive cargo ships DDP

Sensitive cargo most often gets stuck at customs. On DDP we handle export declaration, import clearance, duties, and compliant filing (frozen via food declaration, cosmetics via the relevant registration, pharmacy via dedicated routes) — the recipient just signs. Clearance is steady for general categories with low hold rates, so clients never face customs alone.

FAQ

01 Can I ship frozen food abroad?
Yes. Sausage, frozen dumplings, seafood, and meat are all routine — -18°C cold chain throughout plus food-compliance declaration, delivered to the door in the US / UK / EU / Canada.
02 Can I ship alcohol and sauces like baijiu, soy sauce, hot-pot base?
Yes, via DDP channels. Baijiu, soy sauce, hot-pot base, chili oil and similar all ship; liquids have specific packing requirements — confirm on WeChat before ordering.
03 Can I ship cosmetics, skincare, and perfume abroad?
Yes. Skincare, makeup, and perfume go via the relevant DDP clearance channel; liquids have packing requirements, and we handle destination compliance (e.g. UK) registration.
04 Can I ship pharmacy and supplements?
Yes. TCM, vitamins, supplements, and prescription medicine are supported via dedicated routes. Confirm the specific items on WeChat to be safe.
05 Can I ship branded logo goods?
Authentic logo-bearing branded goods can ship. Tell me the category and quantity on WeChat before ordering so it goes via the right DDP channel.
06 Can I ship lithium batteries, power banks, or magnetic products?
Lithium-battery devices (phones, laptops, power banks) need the model declared in advance via a special channel; pure lithium cells are restricted and generally cannot ship; magnetic and powder items need prior disclosure via a dedicated declared line.
