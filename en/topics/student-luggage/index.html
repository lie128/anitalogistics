Short answer: Air+Truck fits student luggage best

Student luggage is usually a modest volume, you want it to arrive quickly, and you do not want customs chasing you for tax after it lands. Of the four shipping modes, Air+Truck (air freight + destination trucking) fits best: cheaper than international couriers (DHL/UPS), faster than sea, and DDP all the way to your dorm door. Only consider sea or Sea+Truck if you are shipping a large volume well ahead of time.

What can you ship?

Most everyday student luggage is fine: books, clothes for all seasons, bedding / duvets, small appliances like rice cookers, seasonings / dried snacks, skincare and cosmetics. Declare in advance on WeChat: lithium-battery devices (laptops, power banks) need the model disclosed; pure lithium cells cannot ship; magnetic and powder items also need a heads-up.

Why ship DDP (duties prepaid)?

DDP = export clearance + import clearance + duties + door delivery, all covered by the forwarder. The thing students dread most — customs charging extra tax on arrival, or queuing at the post office to collect — does not happen under DDP. The recipient just signs, with no further charges.

FAQ

01 How long does student luggage take to the UK / US / Canada?
Via Air+Truck it is typically 7–12 days to your dorm door: UK 7–12 days, US 7–12 days, Canada 7–11 days. Door-to-door times include export declaration, transit, import customs, and last-mile. Back-to-school and pre-Christmas are peak and may add 3–7 days, so ship early.
02 Can I ship a rice cooker, power bank, or laptop?
Rice cookers and similar small appliances are fine; lithium-battery devices (laptops, power banks) need the model declared in advance on WeChat and go via a special channel; pure lithium cells cannot ship. Books, clothes, bedding, seasonings, and skincare are all OK.
03 I am not in Shenzhen — how do I get my luggage to you?
From any city, courier your luggage (SF Express / JD / Deppon) to our Bao'an warehouse in Shenzhen. Once it arrives I confirm on WeChat and schedule dispatch.
04 Can the luggage be delivered straight to my dorm?
Yes. The Air+Truck line delivers to your dorm / apartment door. Provide the dorm address + room number + recipient name; the truck pre-books a delivery time, so no post-office collection.
05 Will I have to pay extra tax on arrival?
No. It is DDP throughout — duties and taxes are prepaid by us; the recipient simply signs at the destination with no further charges.
